  Data Analysis Questions

1. EMPLOYEE OVERVIEW
--------------------
1. What is the total number of employees in the company?
2. How are employees distributed across the different departments?
3. How are employees distributed by job roles and job levels?


2. EMPLOYEE ATTRITION ANALYSIS
------------------------------
4. What is the overall employee attrition rate, and how many employees left the
   company compared to those who stayed?
5. Which job roles have the highest attrition rates in terms of both number
   and percentage?
6. Is there a relationship between working overtime (Overtime) and employee
   attrition rates?
7. Is there a relationship between the number of previous companies an employee
   has worked for and their attrition rate?


3. SALARY & COMPENSATION ANALYSIS
---------------------------------
8. What is the average monthly income of employees in each department?
9. Is there a relationship between employees' monthly income and their
   attrition rates?
10. Do employees with lower salaries have higher attrition rates when compared
    within the same job level?
11. How do annual salary increases vary across departments and job levels?


4. EMPLOYEE SATISFACTION ANALYSIS
--------------------------------
12. Which departments have the lowest average job satisfaction levels?
13. Is there a relationship between environment satisfaction
    (Environment Satisfaction) and employee retention?
14. Which combination of job satisfaction-related factors, such as
    Job Satisfaction, Environment Satisfaction, and Work-Life Balance,
    is associated with the highest employee attrition rate?


5. CAREER DEVELOPMENT ANALYSIS
------------------------------
15. What is the relationship between the time since an employee's last
    promotion (Years Since Last Promotion) and their attrition rate?


6. EMPLOYEE PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
--------------------------------
16. Which departments have the highest average employee performance ratings
    (Performance Rating)?
17. Do employees with higher performance ratings receive larger salary
    increases?


7. HR RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------
18. What are the top three recommendations that can be presented to the HR
    department to help reduce employee attrition, and what numerical findings
    support each recommendation?
